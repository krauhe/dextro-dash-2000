# Source verification: contraction and GLUT4

Lund S, Holman GD, Schmitz O, Pedersen O (1995).
*Contraction stimulates translocation of glucose transporter GLUT4 in skeletal
muscle through a mechanism distinct from that of insulin.* PMID 7597034.

Verified 2026-09-06 using the [Europe PMC core record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:7597034%20AND%20SRC:MED&format=json&resultType=core).
Local HTTP status 200; title, authors and abstract read. Full text not obtained.

Abstract summary: isolated rat soleus muscle experiments compared insulin and
contraction stimulation. PI3K inhibition reduced insulin-mediated transport
and GLUT4 surface recruitment, while contraction-mediated responses persisted.
Combined stimulation exceeded either alone. The study supports distinct
signaling routes to GLUT4 recruitment. It does not calibrate DEX's uptake rate,
validate human exercise responses, or support treatment recommendations.
