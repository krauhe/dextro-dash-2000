# Background and ground artwork update

1. **Orchard artwork:** built-in image generation re-rendered the existing
   landscape with smoother contours and finer illustrated detail. Saved as
   `assets/retro-landscape-hd.png`; the original remains unchanged. Both early
   stages and the third orchard stage share it. Important limitation: the tool
   returned 1672 x 941, not the requested 3840 x 2160. This is an art-detail
   improvement, not a completed pixel-resolution upscale.
2. **Middle layers:** `scenery.js` adds one code-rendered layer for cellar,
   crystal cave, mountain, volcano, ice, factory and citadel. Each has an opaque
   connected base and theme-specific grounded details. Speed is 0.13 versus
   0.018 for the distant backdrop and 1 for playable terrain. Orchard keeps
   its existing separate transparent PNG layer.
3. **Floors:** small deterministic cracks and worn patches remain within each
   platform's clip rectangle. Stone colours now follow the theme. Collision
   geometry, rewards, enemies and physiology are unchanged.
4. **Validation:** 37 gameplay tests passed. Browser finish tests passed all ten
   crossing positions without page errors, with screenshots of five themes and
   three viewports. Inspected orchard and ice screenshots; reduced the middle
   layer height/density after finding the initial crystal silhouettes too dominant.
