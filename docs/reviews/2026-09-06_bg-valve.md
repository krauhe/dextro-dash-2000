# Connected glucose column and delayed insulin valve

Date: 2026-09-06. Local visual prototype, not committed or published.

## Implemented scope

1. The existing glucose workshop now has a food reservoir directly above a glass BG column, a funnel, falling glucose particles and thin surface ripples. It reuses the game's apple artwork.
2. A visible blue split-gate valve sits below the column. The shutters physically separate, revealing a wider opening; a linked gear changes position. The IOB reservoir has a blue signal cable to the mechanism, not a liquid stream mixing with glucose.
3. Glucose particles move from the valve toward DEX. Smaller independent paths preserve liver supply, background use and renal loss. Exercise is illustrated at the feet; it is not added as another blood-column exit.
4. Main labels retain COB, IOB and true BG. The action label describes the delay without adding a percentage, dose suggestion or treatment instruction. All player-facing text is English.

## Model boundary

The four fixed recordings in `bg-learning-data.js` and both engine files are unchanged. Food is actual D1+D2 gut contents, not the game's separate estimated COB; IOB uses the existing displayIOB. Column height remains BG/22 times 3.8 scene units. Particle flow comes from the existing individual model rates, including signed net blood-to-tissue transport.

Only visual contrast is exaggerated: with a = max(0, x1 / initial x1), t = (a/1.18)^6 and opening = 0.94t/(1+t). This is a bounded drawing function, not a biological valve law or insulin sensitivity calibration. It makes the change in the fixed recordings legible. It is not used to calculate glucose flux, IOB, BG or dosing. Basal activity still produces a partially open valve at zero rapid IOB. Glucose transport and insulin clearance remain independent. Insulin's liver effect remains in the liver-output rate, not a duplicate outflow through the valve.

## Limitations

1. This is a proposed visual explanation, not a literal anatomical system or a learning-outcome validation. The side text and developer notes make that boundary explicit.
2. Particle density and motion are approximate visual encodings, not a mass-conserving particle simulation. Only the unchanged engine determines BG.
3. The small portrait scene prioritises the main amounts and valve; secondary labels are hidden. It is not yet tested as an in-game display during platform action.
4. The original prerecorded apple scenes are retained. Food selection, new physiology, clinical inputs, recommendations and publication are outside this change.
