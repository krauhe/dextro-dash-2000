SUITE 2: ATTRACT AUDIO
----------------------------------------

## [OK] - Browser audio context unlocks
Clicking the attract screen changed data-audio-state to running. The title, credits and demo use the same running music loop and have separate transition cues.

## [NOTE] - Browser gesture is required
Modern browsers block audible Web Audio before the first user gesture. The title screen therefore says CLICK: SOUND and keeps arrow keys as the game-start controls.

----------------------------------------
SUITE 3: BACKGROUND LAYERS
----------------------------------------

## [OK] - Opaque background has no repeated seam
The non-seamless landscape is drawn once with enough horizontal overscan for the complete camera range instead of being tiled.

## [OK] - Transparent layers are grounded
The far and middle PNG layers are cropped to their actual motif bands and overlap the world ground line. No rectangular fill strip or straight alpha transition is rendered.

## [OK] - Parallax order is preserved
The base, far and middle layers use progressively faster camera factors. All remain behind platforms, pickups, enemies and DEX.
